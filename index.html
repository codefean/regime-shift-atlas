About regime shifts

When an ecosystem changes into a different state

Regime shifts are large, persistent changes in the structure and function of ecosystems.

These changes can alter biodiversity, ecosystem services people depend on, and human well-being. The Regime Shift Atlas brings mapped case studies together so users can explore what shifts are documented in their country.

Important context

The figures describe documented records in the Regime Shift Database. They are not a complete national inventory, a current-status assessment, or a country risk score. Differences between countries may partly reflect uneven research and database coverage.
